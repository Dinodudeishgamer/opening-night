# Opening Night

A private app for one group of friends who go to the movies together.

The organizer posts a movie night. Everyone RSVPs, votes on showtimes, confirms the one that gets picked, pays the organizer back for their ticket, locks in a guess of how good the movie will be, and rates it afterwards. Then the app shows how the group's guesses held up.

Opening Night is invitation-only: there is no public sign-up. If you have been invited, the organizer will send you a link and your personal invite code.
