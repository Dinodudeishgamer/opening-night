# Launch checklist

From "everyone is in" to "it's launched". One real movie night, start to finish, with everyone paying attention, is the whole test. Tick boxes as you go (`[ ]` becomes `[x]`).

## Part 1. Tell the testers what to do (5 minutes)

- [ ] Send this to the group chat:

  > You're all testers for the movie club app! Use it for the next movie night like normal. If anything is broken, confusing, ugly, or cut off, text me:
  > 1. What screen you were on
  > 2. What you tapped
  > 3. What happened (and what you expected)
  > 4. A screenshot (side button + volume up)
  >
  > Ideas are welcome too. Nothing you do can break anything.

- [ ] Make one note on your phone called "Opening Night feedback". Paste every report you get into it, and add your own.

## Part 2. Run one real movie night

Each step: what you do, then what to check. Anything that does not match goes in the note.

### 1. Post it (you)
- [ ] Coming Soon → New → search the movie → pick a poster → set the RSVP deadline → create.
- [ ] Everyone got a notification within a few seconds, and tapping it opened the movie night.
- [ ] It is on everyone's Coming Soon, with RSVP under Needs You.

### 2. RSVP (everyone)
- [ ] At least one Going, one Maybe, one Can't Go.
- [ ] Someone adds a guest.
- [ ] Someone who has not answered cannot see who is in; after answering they can.
- [ ] The big card shows faces and the right count.

### 3. Showtimes and voting (you, then everyone)
- [ ] Add your theater and 2 or 3 showtimes.
- [ ] Everyone ticks what works and submits, including for guests.
- [ ] You see who voted; they only see totals.
- [ ] Someone who did not vote got a reminder about a day later.

### 4. Pick the showtime (you)
- [ ] Pick one and set a confirm-by time.
- [ ] People who said Going and ticked it are confirmed automatically and were told so.
- [ ] Everyone else is asked to confirm, and can.
- [ ] The ticket shows the right day, time and theater. Add to my calendar and Directions both work.

### 5. Tickets and money (you, then everyone)
- [ ] Buy the tickets, enter the total.
- [ ] Each person's share is right and they add up to the total.
- [ ] Venmo opens with your name and the amount filled in. Apple Cash opens a text to you.
- [ ] Marking someone paid takes away their "Pay Dino".

### 6. Guesses (everyone)
- [ ] Everyone locks in a guess out of 5. The slider and the +/- buttons feel right.
- [ ] Nobody sees other guesses before locking in their own; afterwards they sit beside the names.

### 7. At the theater (you)
- [ ] Everyone got "is today" or "is tomorrow".
- [ ] Check everyone in. Add a walk-in if someone surprises you.

### 8. After (you, then everyone)
- [ ] End the night. It moves to Past at once.
- [ ] Everyone is asked to rate. The big score reveal plays. Hosts rate their guests too.
- [ ] Nobody sees other ratings before rating.
- [ ] Photos: a few people add some; saving one to the phone works.

### 9. Two days later
- [ ] "How it went" shows the final numbers, with ties shared.
- [ ] Share the recap: the picture looks good in Messages.
- [ ] Past and You tabs updated.

### 10. Once, anywhere
- [ ] Send an announcement with a time limit; everyone gets it; remove it and it disappears.
- [ ] Tap someone's picture: it opens big.
- [ ] Look at every tab in Light and in Dark (You → Appearance).
- [ ] Look for any text that is cut off or squished.

## Part 3. Send it all to Claude

Paste the whole note in one message. For each item, the same four things plus how much it matters:

> **Who / phone:** Gavin, iPhone 13
> **Screen:** Movie night, Tickets card
> **What happened:** The Venmo button opened Venmo but the amount was empty
> **Expected:** $15.50 filled in
> **How much it matters:** Broken / Annoying / Idea
> (+ screenshot)

Also say:
- [ ] Which steps above went perfectly, so they are not re-tested.
- [ ] Anything people asked you ("what does X mean?"): that is something to make clearer.

## Part 4. Launch

The current TestFlight build stops opening 90 days after it was made (about January 8, 2027). Before then, pick one:

- **Stay on TestFlight.** Free and private. Every 90 days Claude makes a new build and everyone taps Update in TestFlight. Fine for a while.
- **Unlisted on the App Store (recommended for a private club).** A normal App Store app that does not show up in search; only people with the link can find it. No TestFlight, no expiry, installs like any app. Needs: a one-time Apple request form, an app listing (name, description, screenshots, a privacy policy page), and one Apple review with a reviewer code. Claude prepares all of it; you submit.

- [ ] Decide which, and tell Claude.
